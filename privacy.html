Seraphym Privacy Policy

Seraphym is a private tool I built for my own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it does: it lets my own assistant on my own laptop read and edit my own Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Calendar, Tasks, Gmail and Classroom when I ask it to.

Who can use it: only me. No one else can sign in.

What it collects: nothing. No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. My sign-in is stored encrypted on my laptop only.

How to stop it: I can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
